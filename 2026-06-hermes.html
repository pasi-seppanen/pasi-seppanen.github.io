Ideas for James (Hermes-Agent on the Helsinki VPS)

June 20266 min readTechnology

Compiled June 2026 from Nous Research docs and the community ecosystem. Tailored to your stack: always-on Hermes service, GPT-5.5 (Pro), Docker-isolated tools, Telegram control. The auto-trader is already your first workload.

The thing that makes James different

Most "agents" answer. James is built around a learning loop: it creates skills from experience, refines them as it uses them, nudges itself to persist what it learns, searches its own past conversations, and builds a model of you across sessions. So the best ideas below aren't one-off tasks — they're recurring workflows that get better the longer James runs. The pattern is almost always the same: channel (Telegram) + a skill + a cron schedule + isolated session.

Start here — low-stakes, high-feedback (given what you already have)

Since the auto-trader already runs on a 15-min cron, you've proven the schedule→skill→Telegram loop. The fastest wins from here:

  • Daily portfolio digest. Wrap your trade.py report in a cron that posts a plain-language morning summary (equity, return %, holdings, any halts) to Telegram.
  • Morning briefing. Weather + calendar + today's tasks + a few headlines, assembled into one Telegram message before you open the laptop.
  • Inbox digest. Every morning James summarizes email, extracts action items, and sends a clean briefing to Telegram.
  • Crypto/tech news triage. Search news on a schedule (e.g. 3×/day), categorize by urgency, and route to a Telegram topic or Discord channel.

Research & knowledge (compounds over time)

  • Paper/citation tracker. James reads papers, tracks citations, organizes findings, and builds a persistent knowledge graph of a domain.
  • Chat with your notes. Auto-contextualize new findings against an Obsidian/Markdown vault; ask questions of your own knowledge base.
  • Topic-isolated sessions. Telegram "Topics" mode gives each topic its own session. Wire an "Engineering" topic to a dev skill, a "Research" topic to an arXiv skill, a "Trading" topic to the auto-trader.

Monitoring & automation (the VPS's natural strength)

  • Price / deal monitoring. Watch listings, flag mispriced items, alert to Telegram on a cron.
  • Competitor / market watch. Weekly scrape of rival sites for price and product changes.
  • VPS self-monitoring. Disk, memory, cert expiry, the Hermes service itself, the trader's cron health — alert if something drifts.

Developer workflows

  • Claude Code orchestrator. Hermes writes prompts for Claude Code over SSH, reviews the output, and routes corrections back — you supervise from Telegram.
  • PR review loop. James reviews diffs and replies in Telegram with suggestions and a merge verdict.
  • Dependency / security scan. Weekly check of project deps for updates and CVEs, prioritized.
  • Build from your phone. Multi-file refactors, CI monitoring, even shipping changes — driven entirely from Telegram chat.

Content & creative

  • End-to-end production pipeline. Drafts, art, TTS narration and a lightweight production workflow.
  • RSS → social. Connect a feed, auto-generate platform-specific post variations.
  • Video handoff. Hand James a screen recording, get back a tutorial video with your preferences kept in memory.

Home & mobile control

  • Home Assistant. Built-in tools to list entities, read state, and call services — conversational smart-home control.
  • Remote Android control. Tools for taps, swipes, screenshots, and screen reading via a relay app — drive your phone from a Hermes chat.

The self-improving / multi-agent end

  • Subagents. Parent agent delegates to isolated child agents running in parallel — real multi-step pipelines.
  • Skill factory. A meta-skill that auto-generates reusable skills from your repeated workflows.
  • Self-optimization. A loop that optimizes James's own skills, prompts, and tool text — producing PR-ready improvements.

A note on safety (matters more because it's always-on)

The same tools that make James useful let it read sensitive files (SSH keys, .env, cookies) and run commands as you, 24/7. You've already done the two big things right — Docker isolation and paper-only trading. Keep using approval modes for anything that touches money, credentials, or external accounts, and keep the blast radius contained per skill.
